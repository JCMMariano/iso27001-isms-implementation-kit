# 🛡️ ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS & TPRM Implementation Portfolio

Welcome to my GRC & Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) practical portfolio. 

This repository demonstrates the design and operationalization of an Information Security Management System (ISMS) aligned with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** and **NIST CSF**, applied to a simulated mid-sized organization (*"TechCorp Solutions"*).

---

## 📂 Repository Structure & Key Deliverables

### 1. 📋 Governance & Policies (`/01-governance-policies`)
* **Top-Level Information Security Policy (Clause 5.2):** High-level policy defining leadership commitment, compliance requirements, and security objectives.
* **Topic-Specific Access Control Policy (Control A.5.15 & A.8.2):** Operational policy defining RBAC, MFA requirements, and access review cadences.

### 2. 📊 Risk Assessment & Management (`/02-risk-assessment`)
* **Risk Assessment Methodology:** Matrix (5x5) defining Likelihood, Impact, Risk Tolerance, and Treatment criteria.
* **Information Security Risk Register (Excel):** Mapped asset-threat-vulnerability pairs, calculated inherent vs. residual risk scores, and assigned risk owners.

### 3. 🎯 Compliance & Auditing (`/03-iso27001-compliance`)
* **Statement of Applicability (SoA):** Mapping all 93 controls from Annex A (ISO 27001:2022), with formal justification for inclusion/exclusion and implementation status.
* **Internal Audit Checklist (Clause 9.2):** Audit protocol designed to evaluate compliance against Clauses 4-10 and Annex A controls.

### 4. 🔍 Third-Party Risk Management / TPRM (`/04-tprm-vendor-risk`)
* **Vendor Security Assessment Framework:** Tiering methodology for classifying third-party suppliers (Critical, High, Medium, Low) based on data access and operational criticality.
* **Vendor Security Questionnaire (Excel):** Standardized security evaluation questionnaire based on Annex A supplier controls (A.5.19 - A.5.22).
